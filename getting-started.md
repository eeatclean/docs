# Getting started

You can try EEATClean on 3 of your own articles, for free and with no card. Here is how it goes.

## 1. Enter your website

On [eeatclean.com](https://eeatclean.com/), type the address of your website, for example `yoursite.com`. There is nothing to install and no plugin to add.

## 2. Create your account

Sign up with Google, or with your email and a password.

If you use a password, we send you a confirmation link. Click it before your first analysis. Can't find it? Check your spam folder, or ask for a new link from the banner in your dashboard. With Google, your address is already confirmed.

## 3. Import your articles

EEATClean then lists the articles of your website. It finds them through your sitemap, or through WordPress if your site runs on it. You choose which content types to import, for example your blog posts but not your product pages.

Importing only reads addresses and dates. It doesn't use any analysis.

If EEATClean can't read your website, you see why right away, with the steps to fix it. The most common causes are explained in [Connect your website](connect-your-website.md).

## 4. Run your 3 free analyses

Pick 3 articles. EEATClean suggests the ones most likely to be outdated, but you can choose any of them.

Each analysis takes a few minutes, because EEATClean checks the facts live on the web. You don't need to stay on the page. The results appear in your dashboard as they finish.

## 5. Read your reports

Each article gets a verdict and, when needed, a list of corrections. Every correction comes with its source. [Verdicts and corrections](verdicts-and-corrections.md) explains how to read them.

Your 3 reports stay in your account, even if you don't choose a plan.

## What next?

To check more articles or turn on [monitoring](monitoring.md), choose a plan from your dashboard. The first month is charged when you subscribe. See [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md).

One free trial is available per account and per website.

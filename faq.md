# FAQ and limits

## Does EEATClean change my website?

No. It reads your public pages and gives you corrections with sources. You decide what to change, in your own words.

## Do I need to install anything?

No. There is no plugin and no code to add. You only enter your website address.

## Which websites does it work with?

Any website with a sitemap, whatever the CMS: WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, Ghost, Drupal and more. If EEATClean can't read yours, see [Connect your website](connect-your-website.md).

## Which languages?

EEATClean analyzes each article in its own language. The app is available in English and French.

## How long does an analysis take?

A few minutes per article, because the facts are checked live on the web. Several analyses run at the same time, and you don't need to stay on the page.

## Is it a fact checker?

Not in the news sense. EEATClean is built for expert articles: guides, tutorials and professional content that cite versions, prices, standards and procedures. It checks whether those facts are still true, or were ever true, and proves each correction with a source.

## What EEATClean does not do

- **It doesn't edit or publish anything** on your website.
- **It doesn't correct without a source.** When no source settles a claim, it leaves the claim alone.
- **It doesn't judge opinions or style.** It checks facts a reader relies on.
- **It doesn't read private pages.** Articles behind a login can't be analyzed.
- **It doesn't replace your judgment.** It can make mistakes, so review important decisions, especially before you delete an article.

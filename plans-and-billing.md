# Plans and billing

You start with 3 free analyses, with no card. To continue, you choose a monthly plan.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Analyses per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | €49 per month | 25 |
| Growth | €99 per month | 60 |
| Scale | €199 per month | 150 |

Every plan includes the same deep analysis, with live sources, the Action Center, [monitoring](monitoring.md) and your full history. Current prices are always on the [pricing page](https://eeatclean.com/pricing.html).

## How billing works

- The first month is charged when you subscribe. The plan then renews every month on that date.
- Each analysis or rescan of an article uses one analysis. An analysis that fails for a technical reason doesn't.
- Unused analyses don't carry over. Your allowance renews at the start of each billing period.
- Payments go through Stripe. EEATClean never sees your full card number.

## Extra analyses

Need more in a busy month? Buy a one-time pack from your account, without changing your plan:

| Pack | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 25 analyses | €59 |
| 100 analyses | €219 |
| 250 analyses | €499 |

Packs are available for a website with an active paid plan.

## Several websites

Each plan covers one website, with its own analyses and billing. To add another website, add it from your account and choose a plan for it. The free analyses are available once per account.

## Change or cancel

- To change plan, contact us at [support@eeatclean.com](mailto:support@eeatclean.com). Your articles, reports and history are kept.
- To cancel, use your account and confirm your choice. No email or call is needed. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and your data stays saved.

The full terms are in our [Terms of Service](https://eeatclean.com/terms-of-service.html) and [refund policy](https://eeatclean.com/refund-policy.html).

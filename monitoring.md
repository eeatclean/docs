# Monitoring

Facts keep changing after you publish: standards evolve, prices change, procedures move on. Monitoring checks your key articles again, automatically, so they keep saying what you would say today.

## How it works

1. In your dashboard, choose the articles to monitor.
2. Pick how often each one is checked: every **1, 3 or 6 months**.
3. On each check, EEATClean runs a full analysis of the article, with live sources.

If a check finds something new, the article goes back to **Needs an update**, with the corrections and their sources.

## Emails

When a monitored article changes, you get a weekly digest email that lists what changed. You get at most one digest per website and per week. You can turn these emails off in your account settings.

## Analyses used

Each scheduled check uses one analysis from your monthly allowance. So plan your monitoring to keep enough analyses for new articles.

Monitoring is part of every plan. The scheduled checks run once you have a plan, not during the free analyses. See [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing.md).
